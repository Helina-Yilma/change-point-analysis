# change-point-analysis
KAIM Cohort 9 Week 10 Challenge
For Birhan Energies, the goal is to detect structural shifts in Brent crude oil prices influenced by geopolitical events, economic shocks, OPEC decisions, and policy interventions. These insights enable:

* Investors to manage risk and optimize portfolios
* Policymakers to plan for economic stability and energy security
* Energy companies to forecast prices, manage operational costs, and secure supply chains
